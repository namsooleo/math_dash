How to Play

Pick the right answer. One wrong answer ends the run.

  • Tap or click an answer. On a phone, tap anywhere on its side of the screen. Keyboard: ← →
  • Survival: 1.5 seconds per question. Each correct answer resets the clock.
  • Sprint: 60 seconds. Answer as many as you can.
  • It gets harder as you go: bigger numbers, then −, × and ÷.

Daily

Everyone gets the same questions today. One try per mode, new puzzle at midnight. Share your scores when you're done.

Freeplay

New questions every run. Play as much as you like; your best score is saved.
